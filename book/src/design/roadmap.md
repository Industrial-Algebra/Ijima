# Roadmap

Ijima is unreleased, in active development toward 0.1.0. The authoritative,
up-to-date roadmap — including shipped phases, the future-feature
explorations (context-poisoning protection, networked-instances
federation), and what remains — lives in the repository:

➡️ **[`docs/ROADMAP.md`](https://github.com/Industrial-Algebra/Ijima/blob/develop/docs/ROADMAP.md)**

In short: the memory palace, knowledge graph, session repository, mining
pipeline, context mapper, and provenance-tier model are merged to `develop`
and tested. Remaining for/after 0.1.0: the backend-sqlite corpus migration
(importing the live pi-mempalace corpus), pi integration, and the Phase 4/5
future features.
